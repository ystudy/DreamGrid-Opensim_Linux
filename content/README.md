Put the starter region at `content/starter-region.oar`. The launcher loads it into the first region when present; otherwise it generates an island.

## Owner plans

Setup starts by asking which kind of world you want. There are five plans:

- **Local** – a private world on this computer, just for you and people on your own network.
- **Grid Asset Server** – your world keeps its items on this computer and can skip regions, so more region computers can join it later.
- **Region Server** – a computer that runs regions for someone else's world.
- **Connected Server** – this computer joins an existing world as one of its servers.
- **Standalone Public** – a world open to visitors from the internet.

Region Server and Connected Server arrive in a later update; their cards show a disabled Choose button for now.

The network step asks whether people reach you by a LAN or a public address. It also has the **hypergrid switch**: turn it on to let avatars travel to and from other worlds. It is off by default, and you can change it later on the Advanced > Plan page.
