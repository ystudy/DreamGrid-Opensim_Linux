# Test model licences

The models in `Models/` come from the Khronos Group's glTF-Sample-Assets (https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets), taken from each model's `glTF-Binary` folder. Only CC0 and CC BY 4.0 models are committed. The larger models that `fetch-models.sh` downloads are not committed and are not covered here.
